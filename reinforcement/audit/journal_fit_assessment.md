# Journal-fit reassessment (Phase 5)

## Constraint conflict, stated openly

* Master Prompt 1 imposed a ≤ 72-day submission-to-acceptance constraint. The previous cycle's
  only verified match was JKSU-CIS.
* The user then asked for "any journal with high impact factor" that would accept the paper, and
  the previous cycle moved to Pattern Recognition.
* **Master Prompt 2 (most recent) reinstates the 72-day hard constraint.** This cycle follows the
  most recent instruction and keeps the Pattern Recognition build as an explicitly
  non-compliant, user-selectable alternative.

## Decision

**SELECT A MORE APPROPRIATE JOURNAL → JKSU-CIS** (returning to the v1 target) under the
reinstated hard constraint. Pattern Recognition fails the constraint on official evidence
(≈ 250 days). It is not removed: its build stays available, labelled **non-compliant with the
72-day constraint**, for the case where the user again prioritizes impact factor over timeline.

Is the paper being downgraded to hide weakness? No. Both journals are Q1 per secondary sources,
and the constraint, not the paper's quality, decides. The scientific reinforcement below is
pursued to the standard of the more demanding venue regardless of target.

**Verdict on the 72-day constraint:** satisfied **at the median** for JKSU-CIS by a transparent
observed sample. A *maximum* of 72 days cannot be verified for any journal, and about 40–43% of
recently accepted JKSU-CIS papers took longer.
