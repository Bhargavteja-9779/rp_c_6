# Conclusion

Duplicate records do not invalidate the marginal guarantee of conformal prediction, but they can redistribute its errors onto new records. The size of that shift is given exactly by the exposure and the over-coverage of seen records. In standard tabular benchmarks the shift is negligible for most datasets but substantial for a predictable minority. A label-free audit identifies the minority, and novelty-Mondrian calibration repairs coverage on new records with a finite-sample guarantee at small cost. We recommend that conformal evaluations on tabular data report duplicate exposure and coverage on novel records alongside the usual marginal coverage.
